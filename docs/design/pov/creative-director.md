# Point of view: Creative Director

North star: a calm command center you want to visit. In 3 seconds the Sr. Manager must see what her team is doing and what needs her now. The world sets the mood. The panel carries the decisions.

## 1. Borrow from the references (patterns, not pixels)

- **Ref 01, 03: the diorama.** A cutaway office floating on a calm backdrop, a "Walk around" pill at the top, a stats card in a corner. Our version: bigger in frame, with ADI blue and warm paper instead of lime green.
- **Ref 04: big number, small mono label.** Our stats: working, on break, needs you, open tasks, handoffs today.
- **Ref 05: chunky characters at real desks.** The big head and small body give charm without any extra color.
- **Ref 02, 06, 07: the team list.** One row per helper, a card that opens to show "Working on", and an empty state that honestly names its data source.
- **Ref 08, 09: the decision card.** Tag, title, who it is from, date, then Approve, Hold, Send back, Instruct.
- **Ref 11, 12: room zoning and light.** Window walls, one landmark per room, navy walls that fit our palette, flat and clean shading.

## 2. Avoid

- **A small building lost in a big empty backdrop (ref 01).** The floor should fill most of the view.
- **Name tags piled on top of each other (ref 01, 03).** At overview, show only the dot and the name. Show the role when zoomed in.
- **A wall of buttons (ref 09).** Twelve cards with four equal buttons each is noise. Approve leads, the others are quieter, and keys A, H, S, I do the rest.
- **Long titles in uppercase mono (ref 08) and raw file paths as titles (ref 10).** Mono is only for short labels.
- **Anything like the HR cards in ref 10.** We never check anyone's hours. The app shows no "online" status, no scores and no rankings.
- **Toy clutter.** No XP, confetti, generic copiers or clocks. A prop must come from a real workflow or add warmth.
- **Their colors, names, characters or layout.** We borrow ideas only.

## 3. Three ideas: premium, cozy, and true to the work

1. **The Endorsement Bridge is the heartbeat.** When a CM helper endorses a PR list, a small blue folder crosses the bridge and an Ops helper picks it up. The "handoffs today" number ticks up at the same moment. This is the real CM to Ops handoff, shown as cause and effect. It is the shot a director will remember.
2. **The Needs You tray and the Mail Dock.** A paper tray sits on the Sr. Manager's desk, and its stack grows with the Needs You count. After she clicks Approve, a sealed envelope drops into the Mail Dock outbox, and it only leaves after the explicit Send click. "Drafts first" becomes something she can see.
3. **A Manila working day.** The light follows the simulated day in Asia/Manila: cool morning, warm afternoon, then a navy night that nods to the "Procurement360" vision. At 3 PM, some helpers drift to Merienda Corner. When a PO is acknowledged, a small truck arrives at the Logistics Window. This is cozy because it is true to the team's day.

Cards speak the team's language: workflow, step, and supplier, for example "PO amendments, step 3 of 4, Luzon Precision". The authority rule line appears when it applies.

## 4. Risks and non-negotiables

Risks
- **Cute can look like a toy in front of a director.** Fix: a strict, flat panel, real workflow words, and fewer props made with more care.
- **26 helpers plus their tags can mean clutter and dropped frames.** Fix: a tag budget, instancing, and a tested overview camera.
- **It might look official.** Fix: no logo or wordmark, fictional data, and "Unofficial prototype · Fictional data" in the About area.

Non-negotiables
- The 3-second test passes on every screen.
- Nothing important lives only in 3D. Every fact is also in the panel.
- Status is never shown by color alone.
- The office shows AI helpers only, never real people.
- No email leaves without a human click.
- 60 fps, no layout shift, and a reduced-motion version of every animation.
- Plain English, sentence case, no em dashes.
