# World artist point of view (P2.1)

## 1. Borrow from the references (patterns, not pixels)

- **ref-01 and ref-03:** the whole office as one small diorama floating on a calm backdrop. A thick floor slab with a visible edge. Open front, two back walls. Off-duty helpers gather in one clear zone (for us, Merienda Corner).
- **ref-05:** chunky, big-headed characters sitting at desks. The charm comes from their size and simple shapes.
- **ref-11:** a big window wall that throws soft light stripes on the floor. Light oak desks. Props packed against the back wall (shelves, files, water dispenser), so the middle stays open.
- **ref-12:** one floor split into rooms: reception with a sofa, a meeting room with a round table, a desk area. Navy walls, which fit our palette. Rooms read by their furniture, not by labels.

## 2. Avoid

- The long, narrow "bowling alley" plan of ref-01. Ours is closer to a square, about 28 x 18 m, so the camera frames it well.
- Tall cubicle dividers that hide the characters (ref-05). Our dividers are 1.1 m at most, or glass.
- Rows of identical cubicles with nothing to find. Every zone gets one landmark you can spot at zoom 20.
- Name pills piled on top of each other (ref-01). Few tags at overview, more when zoomed in.
- The red accent of ref-11, the green backdrop of ref-01, and the logo signs of ref-12.
- Words inside the 3D world that you need to read. Facts live in the panel. The world shows shapes and color.
- Anything that looks like watching people: punch clocks, cameras, "online" lights.

## 3. Three ideas that make it premium, cozy, and true to procurement

**A. The Endorsement Bridge is a real, physical handoff.** A low oak counter sits in the center, between the CM Wing (left) and the Ops Wing (right). A mid blue (#8FB8DD) floor strip runs under it, like a seam. On top sits a two-sided tray rack. A CM helper drops a folder with a gold diamond tab. An Ops helper picks it up, and the tab turns to a teal square. One warm pendant lamp hangs above it. This is the hero of the whole scene.

**B. Props that say "procurement", not "generic office".**
- CM Wing: a supplier sample shelf (parts boxes, cable reels, metal brackets) and a quote board with three cards side by side.
- SAP Station: a PO wall next to a big terminal. Cards move across Released, Acknowledged, Delivered.
- Mail Dock: pigeonholes where envelope stacks grow and shrink with vault events.
- Contract Corner: a signature stand and a stamp. Negotiation Room: a round table with a speaker puck for supplier calls.
- Sr. Manager's Office: a glass corner with a small wall of glowing dashboard panels, a nod to "Procurement360", strongest at night.

**C. A logistics corner and local warmth.** On the right wall, the Logistics Window looks onto a small loading dock outside. There are pallets, a roll-up door, and a small truck from Bayanihan Logistics Services (fictional). When a PO is received, a box rolls in. Merienda Corner gets a capiz-style pendant lamp, a stand fan, a bread basket, and a bench. It should feel like a Manila office pantry, warm and familiar.

## 4. Risks and non-negotiables

- **Performance:** under 150 draw calls and under 250k triangles at the overview. Instance desks, chairs, monitors, and plants. One shared material per color token. Characters about 1.5k triangles each. One shadow-casting key light. Contact shadows rendered once. At most 4 point lights at night, glow comes from emissive materials. No large textures.
- **Readability:** each zone has one landmark and one subtle floor tint. Walkways are at least 1.2 m wide. A main loop path goes Reception, Bridge, Merienda, and back.
- **Palette discipline:** oak floors, off-white walls, navy trim and floor slab edge. Gold (#C9A227) and teal (#1F8A8A) appear only as small track accents: lanyards, folder tabs, desk pennants.
- **Track is never color alone:** star, triangle, diamond, and square badges on every character.
- **Characters:** about 1.5 m tall, head about 40 percent of the height, two dot eyes. Varied, respectful skin tones and hair chosen by seed.
- **Night mode:** navy backdrop #0E1F3B, warm window glow #FFE2A8, desk lamps. Calm, never spooky.
- **Original art only.** No logos, no traced shapes, no copied characters.
