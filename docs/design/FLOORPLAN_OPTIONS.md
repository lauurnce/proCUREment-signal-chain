# Floor plan options, round 2 (P2.4)

Author: world-artist. Status: draft for Lawrence, 26 Sep 2026. Data: `apps/web/src/lab/floorplan/plans.ts`. Inputs: `floorplan/adjacency.md` (procurement-sme) and `floorplan/readability.md` (ux-architect). Floor 28 x 18 m, 26 seats, 13 zones. Back walls are x = 0 and z = 0. The entrance and the loading dock are on the open front edges.

## The one conflict: Leads' Row and the bridge

The procurement-sme wants Leads' Row as a spine along the bridge line. The ux-architect wants 3 m around the bridge with no desks.

**Decision: keep the 3 m clear ring, and keep the SME's split.** No desk sits within 3 m of the bridge center (closest lead: about 3.9 m in Option 1, 5.1 m in Option 2). Leads' Row is still a short, flat row parallel to the handoff, with lead-01 and lead-02 at the CM end and lead-03 and lead-04 at the Ops end.

Why: the bridge is the hero. Its folder slide and its tag need empty floor around them, and the tag math says four seat tags next to it would collide at Overview. Leads right on the bridge would also look like Leads guard every handoff, which is not how the real work goes. One step back, they still see the bridge and sit near their groups.

## Option 1: Central Bridge

**Idea.** The bridge sits at the center of the floor inside a ring walkway. The CM Wing runs along the front left, the Ops Wing along the back right. The CM pennant, the bridge and the Ops pennant land on one flat line across the screen, so a folder moves straight left to right.

- Back corner: Sr. Manager's Office, then the Huddle Room and the SAP Station along the back wall. Contract Corner is on the left back wall.
- Leads' Row sits between her office and the bridge, one step back from the handoff line.
- Front: Negotiation Room next to Reception (visitors walk straight in, no desks on the way). Mail Dock, Logistics Window (dock on the right open edge) and Merienda Corner line the Ops side.

Good at
- Best read at a glance. The bridge is the true center, and the handoff is a flat line on screen.
- Tall rooms (Huddle, SAP, Contract) sit on the back walls. Nothing tall stands in front of a pod.
- The busy Ops route (bridge, SAP, desk, SAP, Mail Dock) stays on the north and east side of the ring. It never crosses the pantry or the visitor path.

Bad at
- Her office is in the far corner, about 20 m from the Mail Dock and the To Planner tray.
- The Negotiation Room sits in front of the bridge. Its walls must be glass or 1.0 m, or it hides the hero.
- Mail Dock and the loading dock are on the right, the side nearest the panel. Keep them low.
- Merienda Corner is next to Reception, a little far from the Huddle Room.

## Option 2: Main Street

**Idea.** One straight street runs from the front door to the Sr. Manager's desk. The CM Wing and the Ops Wing face each other across it. The Endorsement Bridge is the town square where the street meets the cross lane between the wings.

- Head of the street: her office, flat, looking straight down the street at the bridge. Leads' Row flanks the street in front of her, two leads on each side.
- Wings: mirror images, pods of 4, 4, 3 on the left and 4, 4, 2 on the right.
- Front edge, left to right on screen: Contract Corner, Negotiation Room, Reception, Mail Dock, Logistics Window (dock on the front edge).
- Back wall: Huddle Room and Merienda Corner side by side, SAP Station on the Ops side.

Good at
- Easiest to learn. One street, everything opens onto it, and you can walk it with one key.
- Her view is the story: door, square, handoff, her desk. The Leads' split is literal, CM side and Ops side.
- Mirror wings make presets 2 and 3 feel like the same move. Contract Corner and Negotiation Room sit side by side.

Bad at
- The street runs diagonally on screen, so the handoff reads left to right but also slightly downhill.
- The square is open floor, not an object. The bridge prop has to work harder to be the hero.
- Ops walks further: SAP is at the back and the Mail Dock is at the front, so the busiest route crosses the whole wing.
- Merienda Corner sits behind the CM pods, and the back right corner is left empty (plants only).

## Early favorite

Option 1. The flat handoff line and the centered bridge match the brief best, and the busy Ops route is the shortest. From Option 2, borrow the idea that her desk looks straight at the bridge with nothing in between.
