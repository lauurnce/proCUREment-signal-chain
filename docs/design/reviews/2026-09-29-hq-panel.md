## Must fix
1. Collapse the instruction box to a 48 px bar while an item is open, the I key expands it. Keep at least 200 px of Summary above the decisions. (ux-architect, S)
2. Truthful counts and status: the pager counts only the active filter (it said "1 of 4" while the badge said 3). Status "Waiting for a CM decision (demo)". Decides "A Category Manager (you, in this demo)". Rule line "Rule: a price change of 5 percent or more needs Category Manager approval." No seat names (D-046). (ux-writer, S)
3. No dev text in the UI: file paths, "Phase 6", "enabled", "(placeholder)", "Works once the office and the panel are joined". Demo Day "Coming soon." Props "For this visit only. The next reload brings back the saved setup." (ux-writer, S)
4. The Walk around note respects reduced motion: wrap the screen in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`. (motion-designer, S)
5. Switch off state is about 1.3:1 on white: white track, ink-muted border and knob, no knob shadow. (brand-guardian, S)

- Focused card: white fill, one 2 px accent border, no top bar, so Hold stands out.
- Send button: clear enabled and disabled states.
- "2 more below" hint when cards fall below the fold.
- Tab switch resets scroll and fades in over 140 ms. Skeleton tab spacing matches the real tabs.
- Team row detail fades in and lifts 4 px over 140 ms.
- Offline banner shows after 1.5 s of reconnecting, as an overlay fading in over 220 ms.

- q-0003: add PO price, invoice price, extra cost and currency.

## Member reviews (short)

- **ux-writer:** file paths do not belong in visible text. Rule line names a seat and repeats itself. "Coming soon" instead of phases. "Connected · 26 helpers". Friendlier Done today empty state.
- **motion-designer:** calm and on tokens. Walk note ignores reduced motion. Tabs keep old scroll depth, skeleton shifts 4 px on load, Team detail pops in, offline banner flashes on short blips, knob gap uneven.
- **procurement-sme:** realistic flow, authority pass, fiction pass. "Waiting on a decision" is the right name. Wants who it waits on, q-0003 numbers, and the next step after Approve. Privacy: always show "helper" beside seat names, never add done counts per seat.
- **creative-director (first look):** reads as a command center for the first time. Instruction box steals height, focused card too heavy and Hold hard to see, dev wording, "26 seats" vs "26 helpers", sticky header overlap.
