# P2.5 plan: the HQ panel at /lab/panel

## Context
The panel is where the Sr. Manager makes every decision (principle: "the world sets the mood, the panel carries the decisions"). We design it in the Lab with real sample data before Phase 5 builds it for good. We judge it docked next to the P2.3 diorama, the way it will really look.

## Steps

**1. Two council members write the spec (in parallel)**
- The **ux-architect** writes `docs/design/panel/spec.md`:
  - layout and widths from 1280 to 1920
  - every tab and view with its empty, loading and offline states
  - the Needs you card and the item detail view
  - the keyboard map (D-031: keys work only when the panel has focus, never while typing)
  - focus order and WCAG 2.2 AA notes
- The **ux-writer** writes `docs/design/panel/copy.md`:
  - every label, tab name, empty state, button, hint and error
  - the instruction box wording
  - plain English, no em dashes, helpers never people, "CM 03 helper" naming (D-028)

**2. I build it at `/lab/panel`**
- **Page layout:** the P2.3 diorama (look A, day) fills the left side. The panel is docked on the right, about 36 percent wide, with a minimum of 440 px and a maximum of 600 px. It has no shadow and a 14 px corner (D-025, D-033).
- **Over the scene:** the stats HUD from P2.2, with live team numbers, and a "Walk around" pill. Walk mode is Phase 4, so in the Lab the pill only shows a note.
- **Header:** "SignalChain HQ", a live dot and word from the server connection, and "26 on the team" from the seats.
- **Tabs:** Needs you (with its count), Team, Board, Office. Arrow keys move between the tabs.
- **Needs you:**
  - **Cards from the sample queue.** By default the list shows items where the Sr. Manager decides. A "Team items (demo)" filter shows the rest, labeled "Deciding as CM 02 (demo)" (D-027).
  - **Focused card:** it shows the four decisions. Other cards show one "Decide" button.
  - **Item detail view:** the summary, what the helper checked, the options, and the email draft (To, Subject, body) when there is one.
- **Decisions:**
  - A, H, S and I decide, J and K move between cards, Enter opens an item, Esc goes back, and ? shows the key list.
  - After a decision, a 5 second undo bar appears (D-030). Lab only, nothing is written to the vault or sent.
- **Team:** helpers grouped by track, with the status chip, what the helper is working on, and the workflow step. Helpers only, no per-person counts.
- **Board:** the sample tasks in To do, Doing, Waiting and Done.
- **Office:** the zones and camera presets, with a short note on how to move the camera.
- **Instruction box** at the bottom:
  - A "To" picker: one helper, a pod, CM Wing, Ops Wing, or everyone.
  - The button names the audience, for example "Send to CM Wing (11 helpers)".
  - Lab only. It shows what would be sent.
- **Code:**
  - Panel components go in `apps/web/src/ui/panel/`, so Phase 5 can reuse them.
  - The Lab page goes in `apps/web/src/lab/panel/`.
  - Data comes from the store (`/api/state`, which is seeded from `vault.sample/`).
  - Decision state stays local to the Lab.
  - I reuse `DecisionCard`, `StatusChip`, `TrackBadge`, `StatsCard`, `PanelTitle` and `DioramaScene`.
- **Tests:** unit tests for the keyboard logic and the queue filter.

**3. Screenshots**
- `pnpm shots` adds `panel` at 1280x800 (per D-021), 1440x900 and 1920x1080. It shows the diorama and the docked panel together, plus the item detail view and the Team tab.
- I look at every image, fix what looks wrong, then show you.

## Checks
`pnpm check` passes, with no console errors. The keys work with the keyboard only. The office and the other Lab pages do not change.

## Not in this step
No writes to the vault, no email, no walk mode (Phase 4), and no commit until you say OK.
