# P2.4 plan: floor plan options for 26 seats

## Context
Before Phase 3 builds the full 3D office, we pick where every zone and desk goes. The Endorsement Bridge must sit between the CM Wing and the Ops Wing, because it is the real PR handoff. We compare 2 options on screen, with the real workflow routes drawn on them, and pick one.

## Steps

**1. Three council members work together** (two rounds)
- **Round 1, in parallel:**
  - The **procurement-sme** writes adjacency needs: which zones must be close, based on how often each workflow step happens (data/workflows.json) and on the real work.
  - The **ux-architect** writes readability needs:
    - the fixed isometric camera and the docked panel on the right
    - clear walkways at least 1.2 m wide, and one main loop
    - a landmark per zone that is easy to find
    - where the Sr. Manager's Office sits
- **Round 2:**
  - The **world-artist** turns both notes into **2 distinct options**, as exact data (zone rectangles in meters, pods and desk slots for all 26 seats, landmarks, door and walkway points). It goes in `docs/design/FLOORPLAN_OPTIONS.md`, about 28 x 18 m, with every zone from `zones.json`.
  - Working idea for the 2 options, to be refined:
    - **Option 1, "Central Bridge":** the CM Wing on the left and the Ops Wing on the right, with the Bridge in the middle. Leads' Row and the Sr. Manager's Office sit at the back, looking over both wings.
    - **Option 2, "Main Street":** one wide walkway runs through the office, with the Bridge as a plaza on it. The wings sit on both sides, and the stations sit along the street in workflow order.

**2. The diagram at `/lab/floorplan`** (plain SVG, no 3D)
- **Layout:** the two options side by side, or switched with a toggle. Each is a top-down drawing with colored zone rectangles (the approved world zone tints), labels, and small desk rectangles with seat ids (for example "CM 03").
- **Routes for the 6 real workflows** (3 CM, 3 Ops): lines from zone to zone, in step order, along the walkways.
  - "Own desk" means a desk in that track's wing.
  - Each route has a number and a line style, and a legend lets you turn each route on and off.
  - Colors come from the tokens: CM routes use the gold family plus shapes, Ops routes use teal. The lines differ by dash and number, never by color alone.
- **Numbers per option:** the walk length of each route in meters, and the total. This gives a fair, simple way to compare.
- **Code:**
  - `lab/floorplan/plans.ts`: the two options as data, shaped like the future `layout.json`.
  - `routes.ts`: path and length math, with unit tests. For example: every seat has a desk, no zones overlap, every route step lands in a real zone.
  - The page and SVG components.

**3. Screenshots and recommendation**
- `pnpm shots` adds `floorplan-1` and `floorplan-2`. I look at them myself and fix what looks wrong.
- All three members review both screenshots. The world-artist writes the recommendation in plain English in `docs/design/reviews/2026-09-26-floorplan.md`: which option, and why.
- I show you both screenshots, the numbers, and the recommendation. You choose.
