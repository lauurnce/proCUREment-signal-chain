# P2.3 plan: the 3D diorama study

## Context
Direction A (Warm Diorama) looks right in the approved tokens on paper. Now we judge it in 3D, on one small piece of the office, before we build the whole floor in Phase 3. We compare 3 looks, pick one, and check it by day and at night.

## What you will see at `/lab/diorama`
- **The diorama:** a small floating oak slab, about 6 x 5 m. It has the navy edge (D-029), two short back walls, and one window.
- **One desk pod of 4:** 4 desks facing each other in pairs, with chairs, monitors, and a plant.
- **4 helpers, one per track,** seated at the desks: Sr. Manager (star), Lead (outline triangle), CM (diamond), Ops (square).
  - They are chunky and about 1.5 m tall. The head is about 40 percent of the height, with two dot eyes.
  - Skin, hair and clothes are picked by a seed from the approved world palette.
  - Each wears a badge in its track shape and color.
- **Motion:**
  - Idle breathing with a small head sway. Each helper has its own timing, so they never move in sync.
  - Typing: hands tap and the head nods a little. CM and Ops type. Sr. Manager and Lead read (idle).
  - With reduced motion, everyone holds still.
- **Name tags:** the P2.2 `NameTag` pill floats above each head (drei `Html`).
- **A small dev toggle** in the top corner:
  - Look A, B or C, and Day or Night.
  - The same choice also works in the web address, for example `?look=b&time=night`, so screenshots are exact.

## The 3 looks
| Look | Light | Materials and shadow |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Soft daylight** | The skill recipe: sky light plus a warm key light, high from the front left | Matte, soft shadow maps |
| **B. Late afternoon** | Dusk palette, a lower and warmer key from the window side, window light stripes on the floor | Longer, softer shadows. Warm backdrop |
| **C. Clay studio** | Bright, even light, no hard shadow | Navy-tinted contact shadows, slightly more matte, very clean |

- **Night:** navy backdrop, dim sky light, glowing window and screens, and 2 desk lamps (the limit is 4 lights).
- **Where the values come from:** every color is a token from `world` and `worldLight`. Each look is one small data object in `looks.ts`.

## Build (files)
- **The page:**
  - `App.tsx`: new route `/lab/diorama`.
  - `lab/diorama/`: `DioramaPage` (page, toggle, About note), `DioramaScene` (Canvas and camera), `looks.ts` (the 3 looks and night).
- **`scene/world/DioramaSlab.tsx`:** the slab, walls and window.
- **`scene/furniture/`:** `Desk`, `Chair`, `Monitor`, `Plant`, `DeskPod`.
- **`scene/characters/`:**
  - `Character.tsx`: the rig built from rounded boxes.
  - `appearance.ts`: seeded picks, with a unit test (same seed gives the same look).
  - `motion.ts`: pure idle and typing math, with a unit test.
- **`scene/tags/HeadTag.tsx`:** puts the P2.2 `NameTag` above a head.
- **`scene/materials.ts`:** one shared material per color, the rule from `WORLD_PALETTE.md`.
- **`tokens.ts`:** new character motion values (breath about 4 s, typing about 7 taps per second, small amounts). I will list them for your OK.
- **Animation rules:** everything animates in `useFrame` with refs, never React state. In screenshot mode (`?shots=1`), time is frozen, so every image is the same.

## Pick and screenshots
1. `pnpm shots` saves 3 day images, one per look, at 1440x900 and 1920x1080.
2. The **world-artist** looks at them and picks a favorite. It explains why in plain English in `docs/design/reviews/2026-09-26-diorama-study.md`.
3. I set the favorite in `looks.ts`, and the screenshots add its day and night versions.
4. I look at every image myself and fix what looks wrong. Then I show you all of them.
