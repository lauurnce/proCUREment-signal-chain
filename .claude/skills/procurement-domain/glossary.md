# Glossary (from the kickoff workflow boards and program notes)

Plain meaning first. Use these words in the app, and explain them in tooltips the first time.

## Documents and transactions
- **PR (Purchase Requisition)**: the internal request to buy something. Must be approved before it becomes a PO.
- **EREQ**: an electronic requisition. Category Managers approve these.
- **PO (Purchase Order)**: the formal order sent to the supplier: what, how many, what price, when.
- **PR to PO conversion**: turning an approved PR into a PO in SAP. The most repeated Ops activity.
- **RFQ (Request for Quotation)**: asking suppliers to quote a price for a defined need.
- **eRFQ**: the RFQ run through the system instead of loose email.
- **Quotation**: the supplier's priced answer to an RFQ. It has a validity period.
- **SOW (Statement of Work)**: what a supplier will deliver, when, and to what standard.
- **Invoice**: the supplier's bill. Paid by A/P, comes back to Procurement when it does not match the PO.
- **PO amendment**: a change to an issued PO, often because price, quantity or freight on the invoice differs.
- **Credit note**: money the supplier owes back.

## Systems
- **SAP**: the core system of record for PRs, POs, vendors, prices and quantities.
- **ME57**: the SAP transaction to assign and process purchase requisitions.
- **Conga**: the contract tool used to generate SOW and contract documents.
- **DocuSign**: electronic signature and approval routing.
- **Vendor master (master file)**: the SAP record of approved suppliers, including vendor code and currency setup.

## Roles
- **CM (Category Manager)**: owns the commercial side. Decides what is bought, from whom, on what terms.
- **Ops Buyer**: owns the transactional side. Creates POs, confirms them, chases delivery, fixes mismatches.
- **A/P (Accounts Payable)**: the finance team that pays invoices.
- **Planner**: owns demand and quantity. Any quantity change goes to the Planner.
- **Requestor**: the person or department who asked for the purchase.
- **Authorized signatory**: the person allowed to sign for the supplier.

## Sourcing and commercial terms
- **OEM / Non-OEM**: buying from the original maker, or from someone else such as a distributor.
- **Sole source**: only one supplier can fill the need, so it needs its own justification.
- **Vendor identification / qualification**: finding suppliers, then checking they are acceptable.
- **Benchmarking**: comparing a price against the market, past buys, or other suppliers.
- **Bid or quote analysis / tabulation**: side-by-side comparison of offers, usually in Excel.
- **Commercial vs technical offer**: price and terms vs specification and capability.
- **Alignment of quote**: getting the supplier to restate the quote to match what was agreed.
- **Awarding**: telling the winning supplier they have the business.
- **Stock item**: a part kept in inventory and re-ordered regularly.
- **Autosending**: SAP releases the approved PO to the supplier automatically.
- **Dual currency**: a vendor set up for local and foreign currency (vendor account codes to confirm).
- **Endorsement**: the handoff where a CM passes approved PRs to Ops for PO creation.

## Program and vision
- **Procurement360**: the Sr. Manager's vision of one place that connects the helpdesk, savings, projects, policies, contracts and market intelligence.
- **BUC (Bottoms Up Costing)**: the team's should-cost approach.
- **RACE**: the prompting framework the program teaches: Role, Action, Context, Expectation. Context is the slot people skip most. Helper prompts in this app should follow it.
