# Design Council scoring rubric

Scores are 1 to 5. Use whole numbers. Explain any score of 1, 2 or 5 in one line.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Broken or confusing. Blocks the core use. |
| 2 | Works, but a user would struggle or cringe. |
| 3 | Fine. Nothing wrong, nothing memorable. |
| 4 | Good. Clear, pleasant, consistent. Small polish left. |
| 5 | Excellent. Would impress a demanding Sr. Manager and a game designer. |

## Criteria

- **Clarity**: in 3 seconds, can you tell what the team is doing and what needs you? Is the hierarchy obvious?
- **Brand fit**: follows the adi-theme skill: palette roles, type, flat UI chrome, contrast, copy rules, no logo misuse.
- **Charm**: does it feel cozy and alive? Would people smile? Is the charm tasteful, not childish?
- **Craft**: alignment, spacing rhythm, consistent radii and sizes, clean silhouettes, materials, lighting, no visual bugs.
- **Smoothness**: frame rate, easing, response to input, no jank, no layout shift, reduced motion works.
- **Realism** (procurement-sme only): would a real CM or Ops Buyer recognize the work and the flow?

## Severity for fixes

- **Must**: blocks clarity, breaks brand rules, breaks accessibility, or looks broken.
- **Should**: clear improvement to quality or feel, worth doing this phase.
- **Could**: nice idea, park it in the backlog if time is short.

## Effort

- **S**: under 30 minutes.
- **M**: a focused session.
- **L**: needs its own segment or worktree.
